# Welcome — I'm Olivia Rose 


Art has always been the way I make sense of the world.  
I grew up collecting little moments — the way light bends through a window, the quiet hum of early mornings, the stories people carry without ever saying a word. Over time, those moments became sketches, then paintings, then entire collections that I share with you.


## A Little About My Work


Rose's work is rooted in a deep love for the natural world. Every piece reflects her commitment to sustainability, family, and the simple beauty of life outdoors. As a naturally inspired art studio, her designs are created with intention and care — each one handcrafted in small, limited‑edition runs to honor the land, the process, and the people who bring it to life.
